Expectations 
You make a scroll-art java project in a new github repository named scroll-art [example].  
Inside this project, you should have at least two art pieces that run in a loop. 
They can consist of printing moving symbols, or printing out small** ascii still life drawings across the terminal. If you have 2 ascii still lifes, you can combine them in one program. 
One of them must be an ascii art still life (2D). 
**min size: 8*6
max size: most terminals are 120 characters wide, and the default terminal height is around 30. Limit one big still life; we want it to be reusable in other’s programs. 
Your documentation of your process as well as your Citation Points is recorded in your README.md . 
You will generate Citation Points for your code: [From the ASCII art FAQ]
Ultra polite:...ya make yer own ascii and use it.
Very polite:...Ya contact the author and ask if ya can use it...
polite:...Ya use it but you keep the Credits in there like they should be.
rude:...Ya use it and strip credits.
Very rude:...Ya use it and claim that it is Your very own creation…
You must average a 2 or lower on these citation points or below over your two different scroll art submissions, and no 1 program can use more than 4+ points.
If you do more than two scroll art programs, you may take the lowest 2 programs as your average citation  points threshold.  
If you created your own ASCII art, sign your art with your initials.
